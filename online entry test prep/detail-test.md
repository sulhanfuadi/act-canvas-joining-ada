## Test Rules and Instructions:

Log in using the same email address and personal ID number (KTP / Foreign ID / Passport) used during your Academy application form. Please make sure the information entered matches exactly with the details that you provided in your application form. If you encounter issues logging in due to a mismatch in your personal ID information, please log in to your application portal and review the data you submitted.
 
DO NOT click the "FINISH" button before completing all sections.
 
Before starting, click the “Check System Compatibility” button to ensure your device is ready (microphone and webcam access required). Please ensure your webcam is connected and functioning properly, you will be monitored via webcam during the test.
 
You must proceed in the order provided. Click “NEXT” to move to the next section. If time runs out, the section will automatically close.
 
Use the numbered bar at the top of your screen to review or return to questions you’ve answered.
 
All questions are multiple choice. Answers appear on the right-hand side of your screen. Please read the questions thoroughly before selecting the right answer.
 
Scratch paper is allowed. However, opening any unrelated software, applications, or browser tabs may result in immediate disqualification.
 
Ensure that all documents, applications, and browser windows are closed. Also, turn off any notification pop-ups in your system settings. Navigating away from the test page may result in disqualification. This includes switching between browser tabs.
 
Your test will be monitored via webcam, and the recording will be used for documentation purposes. Make sure your face remains clearly visible and centered within the camera frame at all times. Having other people appear in the camera’s view may result in disqualification.
 
If a violation occurs, the test system will notify you and display your remaining tolerance limit. If you receive this notification, adjust your position and continue the test according to the “Test Rules and Instructions.”
 
Choose a comfortable, quiet, and distraction-free location to take the test. You must be alone and should not use mobile phones or tablets during the test.
 
If your internet connection is interrupted, you may continue the test from your last answered question once the connection is restored—as long as there is still time remaining from before the interruption occurred.
 
Once you click the "Start Test" button, you must complete the test in one sitting. The test cannot be paused, stopped, or resumed at a later time. Please ensure that you have sufficient uninterrupted time before beginning.
